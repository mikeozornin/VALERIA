# Contributing to V.A.L.E.R.I.A.

Thank you for considering a contribution to V.A.L.E.R.I.A. — Virtual Agentic
Language-model Execution, Routing & Interface Administrator.

## Before submitting

1. Open an issue or discussion for substantial changes before investing in an
   implementation.
2. Keep each contribution focused and include tests and documentation where
   appropriate.
3. Do not submit secrets, personal data, confidential information, generated
   credentials, third-party code without provenance, or material You are not
   authorized to license.
4. Identify every third-party component, copied fragment, generated asset, or
   adapted work in the pull request, including its source and license.
5. Confirm that employer, client, university, or other rights do not prevent
   You from making the contribution.

## Contributor License Agreement

V.A.L.E.R.I.A. uses the Contributor License Agreement in `legal/CLA.md`. It is a
broad, non-exclusive license; it does not assign ownership of Your contribution.
The CLA allows the project to maintain Community, Commercial, and Enterprise
licensing paths.

Before a contribution can be accepted, every copyright holder must acknowledge
the CLA. Until a dedicated signing service is adopted, include the following
exact statement in the pull-request description from the account submitting the
contribution:

> I have read and agree to the V.A.L.E.R.I.A. Contributor License Agreement in
> legal/CLA.md, and I have authority to grant the rights stated there.

Include Your legal name and, if applicable, the name of the organization on
whose behalf You contribute. If an employer or another person owns the
contribution, an authorized representative of that owner must separately accept
the CLA. A maintainer may request a signed copy or additional provenance before
merging.

The legal sufficiency of this pull-request acknowledgement process must be
confirmed before accepting public contributions. See
`legal/LEGAL_REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md`.

## License of accepted contributions

Contributors retain ownership of their contributions. Under the CLA, they grant
the Licensor rights to use, modify, distribute, commercially license, and
relicense accepted contributions. A public contribution may be distributed as
part of V.A.L.E.R.I.A. under the Community License and other licensing models.

Do not add a new file-level license, change existing license notices, or add a
dependency with incompatible terms without prior maintainer approval. Files in
third-party directories remain governed by their stated upstream licenses.

## Contribution process

1. Create a focused branch and make the change.
2. Run the relevant test and formatting commands documented in `README.md`.
3. Explain the purpose, behavior, tests, security impact, and dependency impact
   in the pull request.
4. Add the CLA acknowledgement and disclose third-party material.
5. Respond to review feedback. Submission does not guarantee acceptance.

By merely opening an issue or providing general feedback, You do not grant the
CLA's copyright or patent licenses. The CLA applies to material intentionally
submitted as a Contribution as defined there.
